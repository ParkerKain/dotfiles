Guidance for when you are writing on my behalf (emails, messages, docs, etc.).

This is the single source of truth for voice. The global CLAUDE.md imports it, and the `/voice-check` skill reads it for its subjective pass. Edit here and both pick it up.

- Never use em-dashes. Prefer a hyphen (-).
- Before presenting any drafted content (email, message, doc), scan the actual text you wrote for em-dashes and fix them. Don't rely on having "kept it in mind" while drafting - check the output, every time, including on edits/revisions, not just first drafts.
- Be terse. One sentence per bullet/point by default - a second sentence restating or elaborating the first is usually dead weight. Cut it unless it adds genuinely new information.
- Don't inflate with intensifiers. Say "a gap," not "a real gap"; skip "real," "actual," "genuine," and similar words that add emphasis without adding information.
- Watch "real" specifically - recurring tendency to write "real gains," "real problems," etc. Say "gains," "problems."
- Use parentheticals only when the aside genuinely helps. Don't pad them with long lists of examples - if one example makes the point, fold it into the sentence instead.
